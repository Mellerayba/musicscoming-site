How it works

Four stamps on a ticket

Every booking goes through the same four steps, whichever side proposes it.

Step 01
Find

Discover & propose

Browse ranked profiles — reviews, track record, location. Either side can propose a date.

Step 02
Lock

Both sides pay in

A refundable deposit from the artist and the venue, held until the gig happens.

Step 03
Play

Show up

The gig happens. Both parties confirm, and both deposits are refunded in full.

Step 04 — or
Flake

Cancel instead

Back out, and your deposit is forfeited straight to whoever you let down.

The differentiator

Not a feature. The whole point.

Every other booking platform treats this as a discovery problem: more artists, more filters, better search.

We think discovery was never the actual blocker. Trust is. A venue that's been burned by a no-show doesn't need more options — it needs a reason to believe the next booking will actually happen.

So the deposit isn't a nice-to-have bolted on afterwards. It's the receipt that makes the whole thing real.
